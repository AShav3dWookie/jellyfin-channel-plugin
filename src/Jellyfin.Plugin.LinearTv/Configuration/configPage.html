Channels

Each channel loops its content on a fixed schedule and appears in Live TV. Tuning in joins whatever is on, part-way through. Changes appear in the guide a few moments after saving.

Add channel

Tick series, collections or whole libraries. To put individual films on a channel, put them in a collection.

Settings

Tuning in with less than this left of a programme starts the next one instead of catching the credits. Zero disables.

How far ahead the guide shows is Jellyfin's own setting: Dashboard → Live TV → guide data days (7 by default, 14 at most).
